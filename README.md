# Kranok, Drawn · กนก วาดด้วยคณิต

Kranok, the flame of Thai ornament, drawn with math, in English and Thai.

https://nanobotco.github.io/kranok/ · https://nanobotco.github.io/kranok/th/

- `tools/copy_text.py` and `tools/copy_math.py` hold all copy (EN + TH); `tools/build.py` writes `docs/index.html`, `docs/th/index.html`, `llms.txt`, `sitemap.xml`.
- `docs/kranok.js` is the geometry: a flame from its bending along the line (log-spiral curl, flat body, reversed flick), notches, the three-figure kranok, the coiled-stem scroll. `docs/app.js` draws every canvas; `docs/three.js` is the three-figure step-through.
- `?card` at 1200×630 renders the share card (`docs/card.jpg`).
- `research/facts.md` holds the sourced notes behind the copy. Photographs come from Wikimedia Commons (public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA), each credited on the page.
